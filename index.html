          What gets checked

          Know what is real.

          “Flash USDT” is not an official Tether token type. A reliable check must match the transaction to the official TRC-20 USDT contract and confirm its on-chain status.

          1. 01
            Transaction record

            Confirm that the transaction exists and succeeded on TRON.

          2. 02
            Official contract

            Match the token against Tether’s TRC-20 USDT contract.

          3. 03
            Transfer details

            Review the sender, recipient, amount, and timestamp.
